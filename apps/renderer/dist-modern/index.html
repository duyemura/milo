Frequently asked questions

I've never done CrossFit. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Every membership starts with a free intro and our Foundations program, where a coach teaches you every movement at your pace before you join group classes.

How do I schedule my free trial?

Book your free intro online — pick a time that works and we'll meet you at the gym for a conversation and a light workout. No pressure, no obligation.

Do you offer drop-ins for visitors?

Yes — experienced athletes visiting Austin are welcome. Book a drop-in online and come train with us.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Any time, no games. Pause or cancel from your member portal or just email us — it's handled within one business day.
